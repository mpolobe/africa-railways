# Token Economics FAQ
## AFC vs AFRC: Payment vs Reward Logic

**Last Updated**: January 11, 2026

## Overview

Africa Railways operates a **dual-token economy** following the industry-standard "Stable Utility vs. Loyalty" model used in sophisticated infrastructure projects:

1. **AFC (Africoin)** - Payment Token (Stablecoin)
2. **AFRC (Africa Rail Credits)** - Loyalty Reward Token

---

## 🪙 AFC (Africoin) - Payment Token

### What is AFC?
- **Type**: Stablecoin (pegged 1:1 with USD)
- **Blockchain**: Sui Network
- **Purpose**: Primary payment currency for railway services
- **Stability**: Maintains consistent value for transactions

### What can you do with AFC?
✅ **Buy train tickets** across all 54 African nations  
✅ **Pay for freight shipping** services  
✅ **Book premium services** (lounges, upgrades, etc.)  
✅ **Cross-border payments** with instant settlement  
✅ **Convert to/from local currencies** (ZMW, TZS, KES, etc.)

### How to get AFC?
1. **Purchase directly** with USD, local currency, or mobile money
2. **Exchange** from other cryptocurrencies
3. **Receive** as payment from others
4. **Top up** via mobile app or website

### AFC Characteristics
- **Stable value**: 1 AFC = 1 USD (always)
- **No volatility**: Price doesn't fluctuate
- **Instant transactions**: Sub-second settlement on Sui
- **Low fees**: Minimal blockchain transaction costs
- **Wide acceptance**: All railway operators accept AFC

---

## 🎁 AFRC (Africa Rail Credits) - Loyalty Reward Token

### What is AFRC?
- **Type**: Loyalty/Reward Token
- **Blockchain**: Polygon (POL)
- **Purpose**: Incentivize network participation and reward loyalty
- **Value**: Market-determined, can appreciate over time

### What can you do with AFRC?
✅ **Earn rewards** for frequent travel  
✅ **Stake for passive income** (12% APY)  
✅ **Vote on governance** decisions (1 AFRC = 1 vote)  
✅ **Receive cashback** on ticket purchases  
✅ **Trade on exchanges** for potential profit  
✅ **Provide liquidity** and earn additional rewards

### How to earn AFRC?
1. **Travel rewards**: Earn 5-10% cashback on every ticket purchase
2. **Sentinel program**: Track workers earn AFRC for safety reporting
3. **Staking rewards**: Stake AFRC to earn 12% APY
4. **Liquidity provision**: Provide liquidity on DEXs
5. **Governance participation**: Active voters receive bonus AFRC
6. **Referral program**: Invite friends and earn AFRC

### AFRC Characteristics
- **Variable value**: Price determined by market supply/demand
- **Deflationary**: 1% burn on freight transactions
- **Governance rights**: Vote on protocol decisions
- **Staking rewards**: Earn passive income
- **Limited supply**: 1 billion total (fixed)

---

## ⚠️ Critical Distinction: What You CANNOT Do

### ❌ You CANNOT pay for tickets with AFRC
**Why?** AFRC is a loyalty reward token, not a payment currency. Its value fluctuates, making it unsuitable for stable pricing.

**Example**:
- ❌ WRONG: "I want to buy a $25 ticket with AFRC"
- ✅ CORRECT: "I want to buy a $25 ticket with AFC (or 25 AFC)"

### ❌ You CANNOT convert AFRC directly to AFC
**Why?** These are separate tokens with different purposes. AFRC must be traded on exchanges first.

**Correct Process**:
1. Trade AFRC for USD/USDT on exchange
2. Use USD/USDT to buy AFC
3. Use AFC to buy tickets

## 💡 Economic Logic: Why Two Tokens?

### The "Stable Utility vs. Loyalty" Model

This dual-token system is used by successful infrastructure projects worldwide:

#### Payment Token (AFC) Benefits:
- **Price stability** for merchants and customers
- **Predictable costs** for budgeting and planning
- **Regulatory clarity** as a stablecoin
- **Wide adoption** due to no volatility risk
- **Instant settlement** without exchange rate concerns

#### Reward Token (AFRC) Benefits:
- **Value appreciation** potential for loyal users
- **Governance participation** for community
- **Staking rewards** for long-term holders
- **Deflationary mechanics** create scarcity
- **Speculative upside** attracts investors

### Real-World Analogy
Think of it like airline miles:
- **AFC = Cash** - You pay for flights with cash (stable, predictable)
- **AFRC = Miles** - You earn miles as rewards (valuable, but can't directly buy tickets)

## 🔄 Token Conversion Guide

### How to Convert Between Tokens

#### AFC → Local Currency
```
1. Open Africa Railways app
2. Go to "Wallet" → "Withdraw"
3. Select local currency (ZMW, TZS, etc.)
4. Enter amount
5. Receive via Mobile Money or Bank Transfer
```

#### Local Currency → AFC
```
1. Open Africa Railways app
2. Go to "Wallet" → "Top Up"
3. Select payment method (Mobile Money, Card, Bank)
4. Enter amount
5. Receive AFC instantly
```

#### AFRC → Local Currency
```
1. Go to exchange (e.g., Cetus DEX, Uniswap)
2. Trade AFRC for USDT/USD
3. Withdraw USDT to local exchange
4. Convert to local currency
5. Withdraw to bank/mobile money
```

#### Local Currency → AFRC
```
1. Buy USDT with local currency
2. Send USDT to exchange
3. Trade USDT for AFRC
4. Withdraw AFRC to wallet
```

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why can't I just use one token for everything?
**A**: Using two tokens provides:
- **Stability** for payments (AFC)
- **Growth potential** for rewards (AFRC)
- **Regulatory compliance** (stablecoins have clearer regulations)
- **Better user experience** (predictable ticket prices)

### Q: What happens to my AFRC if I don't use it?
**A**: AFRC remains in your wallet indefinitely. You can:
- Stake it for 12% APY
- Hold it for potential appreciation
- Trade it on exchanges
- Use it for governance voting

### Q: Can AFRC ever be used for tickets?
**A**: No. AFRC is designed as a reward token only. This separation maintains economic stability and regulatory compliance.

### Q: How is the AFRC price determined?
**A**: AFRC price is determined by:
- Market supply and demand
- Trading volume on exchanges
- Network growth and adoption
- Staking participation rate
- Deflationary burn mechanism

### Q: Is AFC really always $1?
**A**: Yes. AFC is a stablecoin pegged 1:1 with USD through:
- Collateral reserves
- Algorithmic stability mechanisms
- Regular audits
- Redemption guarantees

### Q: What if AFC loses its peg?
**A**: AFC has multiple stability mechanisms:
- Over-collateralization
- Arbitrage opportunities
- Emergency reserves
- Insurance fund

### Q: Can I earn both AFC and AFRC?
**A**: You can:
- **Earn AFC**: Receive as payment, sell goods/services
- **Earn AFRC**: Travel rewards, staking, Sentinel program

### Q: Which token should I hold long-term?
**A**: Depends on your goals:
- **Hold AFC**: If you need stable value for upcoming travel
- **Hold AFRC**: If you want growth potential and staking rewards
- **Hold both**: Balanced approach (AFC for spending, AFRC for rewards)
