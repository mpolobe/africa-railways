## The Backbone Is Back

The Tanzania-Zambia Railway Authority (TAZARA) has been a lifeline for landlocked Zambia since 1976, providing access to the Indian Ocean through Tanzania's Dar es Salaam port. For decades, it carried copper exports, regional trade, and the hopes of two nations.

But aging infrastructure, manual operations, and financial constraints took their toll. Services became irregular. Equipment deteriorated. Cross-border friction increased.

**Until now.**

### The Resumption: February 10, 2026

The Mukuba Service—named after the Bemba word for "copper"—is officially back on schedule:

- **Route**: New Kapiri Mposhi (Zambia) → Dar es Salaam (Tanzania)
- **Distance**: 1,860 kilometers
- **Service**: Cross-border passenger transport
- **Frequency**: Regular scheduled service
- **Challenge**: Fleet availability constrained by aging equipment

This isn't just a railway reopening. This is the beginning of Africa's railway renaissance.

And **Sentinel ($SENT) is powering the digital infrastructure that makes it possible.**
