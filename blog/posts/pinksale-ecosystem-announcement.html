Why SENTINEL?

Unlike typical meme tokens or speculative projects, SENTINEL ($SENT) is backed by real infrastructure. The token powers governance and fee-sharing for Africa Railways—a blockchain-based railway management system already piloting on the TAZARA corridor connecting Tanzania and Zambia.

Key differentiators include:

  • Real Utility: Governance rights over railway protocol decisions
  • Fee Sharing: Stakers earn from platform transaction fees
  • Proof-of-Safety: Track workers earn $SENT for verified safety reports
  • Live Pilot: Already operational on 1,860km TAZARA railway
