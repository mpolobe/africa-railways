One-Time Setup (5 minutes)

  1. Open Telegram and message @BotFather
  2. Send /newbot and follow prompts to create your bot
  3. Copy the API Token (looks like 123456:ABC-DEF...)
  4. Create a Telegram Channel for announcements
  5. Add your bot as an Administrator to the channel
  6. Get your channel ID: forward a message from your channel to @userinfobot
  7. Enter credentials below and click "Save Configuration"
