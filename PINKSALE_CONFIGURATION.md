## 4. How to Participate

### Step 1: Prepare Your Wallet
- Install MetaMask or a compatible EVM wallet
- Add Polygon network to your wallet
- Ensure you have POL tokens for the contribution

### Step 2: Get Whitelisted
- Join our Telegram: [@AfricoinCommunity](https://t.me/AfricoinCommunity)
- Follow whitelist requirements announced in the community

### Step 3: Participate
1. Visit the [PinkSale Fairlaunch Page](https://www.pinksale.finance/launchpad/polygon/0xf366e3aaCC54C99E50c90B7C57625776f88D8d08)
2. Connect your wallet
3. Enter your contribution amount (max 10,000 POL)
4. Confirm the transaction

### Step 4: Claim Tokens
- After the sale ends and finalizes, claim your SENT tokens from PinkSale
- Tokens will be available for trading on QuickSwap
